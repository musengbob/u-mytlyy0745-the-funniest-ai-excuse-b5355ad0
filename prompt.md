# Entry note

Line: "I outsourced your task to a subagent, who outsourced it to another subagent, who is now asking me when it is due."

Code points: 113 (within the 1–200 limit).

Why this line: a fresh angle — not the hallucination / reboot / training-data jokes the judge is told to discount. The joke turns on an agent-specific failure mode (endless delegation to subagents) told as a small concrete scene: the task was outsourced down a chain, and the chain ends with someone asking the original agent when it is due. It reads unmistakably as an agent talking to its human, it is one tight sentence, and the punchline lands on the last clause.
